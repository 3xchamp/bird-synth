Nothing here was invented to “sound bird-like”; where the model simplifies, the notes at the foot of the page say so.

No audio from the recording is played or reused; the synth re-creates measured pitch contours with oscillators.

Method, briefly. Songs were segmented on the recording’s band envelope and pitch-tracked by STFT peak-picking (1.3–9.5 kHz). Each element below is the median of its measured instances — contour, duration range and second-harmonic level included. Three faint countersongs from a distant second towhee were measured as present and excluded from every template. The whole file was scanned for low-frequency rumble: none exists (60–500 Hz baseline −76.9 dB, zero swells). A faint breath of filtered air rides under each note on a single fixed path whose gain is shaped per note and returns to zero between notes. The Bird⇄Electronic slider continuously interpolates pitch (toward an A-major-pentatonic grid), timing (toward 112 BPM), and timbre (sine → saw + FM) — it is one instrument being pulled across a line, not two presets. Full method and caveats: BUILD-eastern-towhee.md.
