Measured phrase types — hear each one alone

Source. Modeled from xeno-canto recording XC75501 — Northern Cardinal, recorded by Jonathon Jongsma, Wood Lake, Richfield, Minnesota, 2011-04-09 · CC BY-SA 3.0. Jimmy picked this recording himself after the first Gate 1 build failed his ear-gate; by measured phrase-to-floor contrast it is the cleanest file of the candidate set. No audio from the recording is played or reused; the synth re-creates measured pitch contours with oscillators.

Method, briefly. Notes were segmented on the recording’s band envelope and pitch-tracked by STFT peak-picking (1.3–9.5 kHz). Each phrase type below is the median of its measured instances — contour, duration range and second-harmonic level included. The whole file was also scanned for the aircraft/vehicle rumble Jimmy caught by ear in the retired Kentucky reference: no low-frequency swell exists in this file (BUILD.md has the scan). The two syrinx voices alternate the low and high notes (one voice at a time, as the recording shows); a faint breath of filtered air rides under each note on a single fixed path whose gain is shaped per note and returns to zero between notes. The Bird⇄Electronic slider continuously interpolates pitch (toward an A-major-pentatonic grid), timing (toward 112 BPM), and timbre (sine → saw + FM) — it is one instrument being pulled across a line, not two presets. Full method and caveats: BUILD.md.
