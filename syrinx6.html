▸ NORTHERN CARDINAL
Voices, measured — not sampled. Every note on this page is synthesized from pitch contours, amplitude envelopes and timing measured off a single reference recording per bird; no recording audio is used or played. Northern Cardinal — XC75501, Jonathon Jongsma, CC BY-SA 3.0 · Great Horned Owl — XC11638, Nathan Pieplow, CC BY-NC-ND 2.5 · Hermit Thrush — XC1048898, AUDEVARD Aurélien, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · Carolina Wren — XC364592, Brian Murphy, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · Song Sparrow — XC1005250, Ryan Douglas, CC BY-SA 4.0 · Eastern Towhee — XC1015083, Stanislas Wroza, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 · Recordings via Xeno-canto. The Bird⇄Electronic morph (per-channel knob + master) pulls pitch to an A-major-pentatonic grid and timing to the TEMPO grid, and crossfades each voice toward raw synth.
