Bird-synth engine · Hermit Thrush

Hermit Thrush Catharus guttatus

A syrinx, modeled — not sampled. Every pitch curve, note length and pause in this instrument was measured from one reference recording: 30 notes pitch-tracked frame by frame, 29 of them templated into seven phrase types across 8 songs. This bird's problem is pitch level: each song is sung at its own height — the measured ladder runs 2766 · 3234 · 3539 · 3727 · 3750 · 3797 · 3867 · 4172 Hz — and every sustained note carries a measured shimmer, an amplitude pulse at a median 11 Hz. Nothing here was invented to “sound bird-like”; where the model simplifies, the notes at the foot of the page say so.

Modeled from xeno-canto recording XC1048898 — Hermit Thrush, recorded by AUDEVARD Aurélien, Homer, Kenai Peninsula, Alaska, 2022-06-20 · Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 · XC grade A (verified on the recording page). Background species on the recording page: none. By measured phrase-to-floor contrast (gap floor −46.3 dB RMS vs phrases −21.5 dB, p10/p90 of 0.25 s windows) the phrases stand well clear of a near-silent floor. No audio from the recording is played or reused; the synth re-creates measured pitch contours with oscillators. No recording audio is used anywhere in this page.

Method, briefly. Notes were segmented on the recording’s band envelope in two passes (a body pass and a prominence-gated sensitive pass) and pitch-tracked by STFT peak-picking (1.3–9.5 kHz). Each phrase type below is the median of its measured instances — contour, duration range, harmonic levels and shimmer rate included. The whole file was scanned for aircraft/vehicle rumble: no low-frequency (60–500 Hz) swell exists in this file (see BUILD-hermit-thrush.md). A faint breath of filtered air rides under each note on a single fixed path whose gain is shaped per note and returns to zero between notes. The Bird⇄Electronic slider continuously interpolates pitch (toward an A-major-pentatonic grid), timing (toward 112 BPM), and timbre (sine → saw + FM) — it is one instrument being pulled across a line, not two presets. Full method and caveats: BUILD-hermit-thrush.md.
